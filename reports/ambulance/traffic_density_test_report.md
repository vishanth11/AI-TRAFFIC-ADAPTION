# SUMO Traffic Density & Ambulance Corridor Report

**Goal**: Adjust the normal vehicle traffic to a LIGHT/MODERATE density (~5 vehicles per route) while preserving the ambulance corridor functionality in the existing `corridor_emergency.sumocfg` scenario.

## Modifications Made
- **File Modified**: `simulation/routes/corridor_emergency.rou.xml`
